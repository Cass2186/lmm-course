3 · Modelling the RCT

Group × time, emmeans contrasts, baseline adjustment and dropout

Course data (loads automatically; click to see the code)

R runs inside your browser. The first visit downloads R and the packages, which takes 20–40 seconds; after that the browser caches them. Wait for this block to finish before running exercises.

Primary estimand: the difference between Protein and Placebo in mean change in VL CSA from week 0 to week 12, in all randomized participants.

With a few fixed visits, treat time as a factor (Week_f). The primary model is

fit <- lmer(VL_CSA_cm2 ~ Group * Week_f + Sex + (1 | ID), data = csa)
Coefficient Meaning
GroupProtein Protein − Placebo at week 0 (chance only, by randomization)
Week_f6, Week_f12 Change from week 0 in Placebo
SexM M − F (the stratification factor)
GroupProtein:Week_f12 Extra change in Protein by week 12: the estimand

emmeans gives the same number with clearer labels. Note that emmeans uses Kenward–Roger df, while summary() uses Satterthwaite; they agree closely here.

Explore: five ways to answer the trial question

Pick an outcome. Each row is a different analysis of the week-12 treatment effect, with its 95% CI. Only some of them estimate the estimand efficiently.

viewof outcome = Inputs.select(
  new Map([["VL CSA (cm²)", "csa"], ["Leg lean mass (kg)", "lean"], ["Leg press 1RM (log scale)", "rm"]]),
  {label: "Outcome"})

The “arms at week 12 only” row is a between-person comparison, so its CI is ~3× wider and it includes the chance baseline imbalance. The difference-in-change, cLDA and ANCOVA analyses all use each person as their own control and agree closely, as they should in a randomized trial. For 1RM the estimates are log ratios; exp() them for fold-change ratios.

Exercises

Q1 — Fit and read the primary model

fit <- lmer(VL_CSA_cm2 ~ Group * Week_f + Sex + (1 | ID), data = csa)

GroupProtein:Week_f12 ≈ 0.99 (SE 0.28, p ≈ 0.0008): Protein gained ~1 cm² more than Placebo by week 12. Week_f12 ≈ 1.05 is Placebo’s own gain from training. The omnibus Group:Week_f F-test (F ≈ 6.20, p ≈ 0.003) asks whether trajectories differ at all; report the specific week-12 contrast with its CI instead.

Q2 — Changes and the difference in change

Get each arm’s change from baseline, then the Protein − Placebo difference in change.

"revpairwise" gives Protein − Placebo; "pairwise" would give Placebo − Protein.

Change wk 0→6 Change wk 0→12
Placebo 0.64 [0.24, 1.03] 1.05 [0.66, 1.43]
Protein 1.18 [0.79, 1.58] 2.04 [1.63, 2.45]
Protein − Placebo 0.55 [−0.01, 1.10] 0.99 [0.43, 1.55]

That’s ≈ 4% of the baseline mean CSA (24.8 cm²).

Q3 — Constrained baseline (cLDA)

Fit cLDA by replacing the Group main effect with hand-made indicators, so both arms share one baseline mean.

Prot_wk12 ≈ 1.03 (SE 0.28), almost identical to the unconstrained 0.99 (0.28) and ANCOVA 1.03 (0.29). cLDA partially corrects the chance ≈ 0.9 cm² baseline imbalance.

Q4 — Completers only

Refit the primary model using only the 35 participants with all three visits. Compare with the all-randomized estimate.

Completers: 0.90 (SE 0.30, 95% CI 0.30 to 1.50) vs all randomized 0.99 (SE 0.28, 0.43 to 1.55). The dropout here was random, so they agree. The all-data LMM assumes missing at random; it would be biased if, say, Protein participants who weren’t growing were more likely to quit.

Q5 — Factor or linear time?

LRT χ²(2) ≈ 1.34, p ≈ 0.51, and AIC favours linear time by ≈ 2.7; the linear model’s 12-week difference is ≈ 1.00 (SE 0.28). Still, don’t switch the primary analysis after seeing the data; report linear time as supportive.
