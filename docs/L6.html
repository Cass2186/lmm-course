Did your 95% CIs contain the true values? The CSA estimate (≈ 0.99) is larger than the truth (0.72), although its CI (0.43 to 1.55) covers it. That’s ordinary sampling variation in one trial of 40 people, and a reminder to plan the next study’s sample size around a conservative effect, not the last point estimate.
